## Classic Studio v1.0.0

A client-side 3D configurator for planning mods to an iPod Classic A1238 (6th and 7th generation). Pick parts, see them on a rotatable model, check compatibility, and export a priced parts list.

Live app: https://classic-studio-kyle.kyle-gothman.chatgpt.site

### Highlights

- Rotatable 3D iPod with physically based materials, a studio HDR environment, screen glass, SSAO, selective screen bloom, and an exploded and x-ray view of the internals.
- Procedurally built one-piece back shell in thin (10.5 mm) and thick (13.5 mm) bodies, with 30-pin, USB-C, and Classic Connect 2 port layouts cut into the shell. Shells are baked at build time, so there is no CSG at startup.
- Full Elite Obsolete Electronics color catalog for the A1238: 57 faceplates, 56 click wheels, 38 center buttons, 28 back plate colors in every factory engraving style, 31 dock bezels, and 5 hold-switch assemblies, grouped by finish and searchable.
- Compatibility engine with inline fixes: 128GB LBA28 cap on 6th and 6.5th gen boards, thin-body restrictions for Bluetooth and USB-C kits, AirTag, and thick-format batteries, EOE thin-battery adapter rules (iFlash Quad and uDUAL only), Classic Connect 2 enclosure rules, and material pairing for faceplates and buttons.
- Runtime estimate under the battery picker based on storage type, firmware, and Bluetooth.
- Parts list with editable prices, bundle toggles for center buttons and preinstalled jack and bezel, category subtotals, copy as text, and CSV export.
- Build guide with a suggested install order, difficulty per step, and a time allowance.
- Presets (Stock restore, Daily driver, Audiophile), shareable build links that encode the whole configuration, and migration for links saved by earlier versions.
- Portable single-file build (`outputs/classic-studio.html`) for offline use, generated by `build-single.py`.
- Two test suites: a compatibility matrix over every option and a geometry test that measures the real GLB and ray-casts through the baked shells to verify the port openings.

### Notes

Prices are planning estimates in USD as of September 2026, not live quotes. Internal component placement is schematic and does not establish mechanical fit. Wi-Fi is intentionally unavailable because no such mod exists for the stock logic board.
